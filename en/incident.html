Personal Data Incident — Response Draft Generator

For non-government agencies | Based on the PDPA and its Enforcement Rules currently in force, and on the Executive Yuan Directions (unofficial translation)

Incident details

Six questions, plus two optional ones at the end. The draft appears on the right as you answer. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

01When you became aware of the incident

The deadlines in the Directions are counted in hours and run continuously; they do not pause for weekends or holidays. Enter the time to the minute.

02Is this a major and high-profile case?

Point 2, Paragraph 2 of the Directions: a case that has attracted the attention of the Executive Yuan, the Legislative Yuan or the Control Yuan, or one covered in the national pages of print media or discussed as a feature in electronic media. Answering yes switches the competent authority's deadline from 72 hours to 24 and moves the investigation to a 3-day and 10-day rhythm.

03Form of infringement (select all that apply)
04Does it involve Article 6 personal data?

Medical records, healthcare, genetics, sex life, physical examination and criminal records (PDPA Article 6, Paragraph 1).

05Is your organisation a specific non-government agency under the Cyber Security Management Act?

A critical infrastructure provider designated by the competent authority. If so, the cybersecurity reporting procedure must be started in parallel.

06Have the facts of the infringement been clarified?

The duty to notify data subjects under Article 12 of the PDPA arises once the facts have been clarified.

07Which authority regulates your organisation? optional

Leave blank if you do not know. The draft still works; the prompt will simply tell the assistant how to proceed without it. The full index is in 02 Sources and Terminology, Part E.

08Which sector regulation applies? optional

Select an authority above to see its regulations. Leave blank if you do not know or none applies.
