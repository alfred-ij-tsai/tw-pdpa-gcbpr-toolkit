For non-government agencies | Based on the provisions of the PDPA and its Enforcement Rules currently in force

Case details

Five questions. The draft appears on the right as you answer. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

01Date the request was received

Periods run from the following day; the day of receipt is not counted.

02Type of request (select all that apply)
03Has the requester's identity been verified?
04Do you intend to refuse?
05Are you extending the decision period?

An extension may be granted once only: up to a further fifteen days for inquiry, review and copies; up to a further thirty days for supplement, correction, cessation and erasure. In either case the reason must be notified to the requester in writing.
